System Policy & Security Controls
Mandatory Separation of Duties (Dual-Control Policy): Under cooperative financial regulations, the officer who initiates or prepares a financial transaction (loan application, withdrawal request, or payroll adjustment) is strictly prohibited from approving the same transaction.
